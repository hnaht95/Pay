# Kháng nghị 5.1.1(ix) - Payso 1.0

## 1. Thư trả lời App Review (tiếng Anh)

Hello App Review team,

Thank you for reviewing Payso. We would like to ask you to reconsider the 5.1.1(ix) decision, because Payso is a personal expense tracker, not a financial or payment service.

What Payso does:
- Users record their own spending (by voice, keypad or receipt photo) and see statistics. All data stays on the device or in the user's own iCloud.
- When the user scans a VietQR code (the national QR standard printed at Vietnamese shops), Payso only reads the recipient and amount from the code, records the expense, and then opens the user's own banking app through a standard URL link with those details prefilled. This is the same as copying an account number into the banking app.

What Payso does not do:
- It does not hold, move, process or transmit money. Every transfer is made and confirmed by the user inside their bank's own app.
- It does not connect to any bank account, does not ask for bank credentials, card numbers or any login, and has no server of its own.
- It does not offer loans, investing, crypto, insurance or any other regulated financial service.
- The shared group feature only calculates who owes whom; members settle up in their own banking apps.

Payso is in the same category as other expense tracker and budgeting apps on the App Store. We have also updated the app description and subtitle to make this clearer.

Thank you for your time.
Thanh Nguyen
